Courts and routes

Simple and honest: we show the places, people agree who books and who pays, and they play. Three journeys: a court we can't see into, a run with its route, and picking where to play.

1 · Courts

Two kinds of courts, one rule: the host owns the booking

Book in the appVenues that work with us (Andorra today). Free and taken times are live; booking makes the session. Nothing changes.
Book with the venueEvery other court. We list it with its phone and booking link. The host books it there, then says so in the app.
Everyone sees the deal"Court booked by Riad · SAR 60 each, pay at the venue" shows before anyone taps I'm in. No surprises at the court.

2 · Running routes

Every run has a route on a Beast Tribe map

The map is ours: the slate board for streets, Operation Beast orange for the route, aqua for the meeting point. A route is drawn once and reused: popular routes in your city come first, and anyone hosting can draw a new one.

3 · Choosing where

Popular for your sport, near you, on one map

Same idea for every sport. Pick the sport, and the Where step opens on a map of your city with the places people actually play it, most used first. Courts show Live times or Venue; parks and tracks show their routes. Nothing fits? Drop a pin.

What it takes

How we'd build it

Courts (no new app build)

  • Places get a "how to book" field: in the app (live) or with the venue, plus phone and booking link. Staff fill it in the dashboard.
  • Sessions get: court booked yes/no, court price (optional). The split and "pay at the venue" reuse what Andorra already does.
  • Reminders: host nudge the evening before; heads-up to players 3 hours before if still not booked.
  • Ships as a normal update in days.

Routes and maps (one new app build)

  • A real map in the app needs a map component installed, so one new build for iPhone and Android (TestFlight again).
  • Brand map style: MapLibre with our own teal and orange style, on OpenStreetMap data. Free to start; a tile host costs a little once usage grows.
  • Routes are saved lines with distance, start point and city; popular ones rise to the top.
  • Until the build is out, sessions show the route as a picture, so nothing waits.

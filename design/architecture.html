Beast Tribe · October 2026

How Beast Tribe works

Three screens, one shared database, and a few helpers. Everything people do in one place shows up in the others within seconds.

1 · The big picture

Three doors, one brain

The appMembers on iPhone and Android: find, join and host sessions, groups, courts, feed.
Partner dashboardGyms, companies, schools, venues, coaches: classes, courts, members, challenges.
Staff dashboardThe Beast Tribe team: leads, partners, communities, captains, safety.
One database (Supabase)Accounts, sessions, communities, groups, courts, photos. Its own rules decide who can see each row. Hosted in Mumbai.

Helpers it talks to

Push notifications (Expo) Ask Beast (Claude) Apple Health (steps only) Phone location (city only) Photo check before it shows Scheduled jobs (reminders)

A gym adds a class in its dashboard, the database saves it, and members' Boards show it. Nothing is copied between the three screens; they all read the same database.

2 · Where each piece lives

Three places in the cloud

  • English and Arabic, right-to-left
  • Tabs: Board, Explore, Tribe, You + Play

Built with Expo (React Native). Store builds via TestFlight and Google Play.

The website

  • Partner and staff dashboards
  • Public pages: for gyms, companies, leaders

Next.js on Vercel · beast-tribe.vercel.app

The database

  • Sign-in and accounts
  • All data, plus photos in storage
  • Rules on every table and column
  • Jobs on a timer: captain nudges, photo reminders, clean-up

Supabase (Postgres) · Mumbai region

3 · How things fit inside each other

Communities hold everything

Partner gym, company, school, venue, coach

Owns a private community and runs it from the partner dashboard.

Beast Tribe staff

Create communities, add leaders and captains, verify clubs.

Community the organisation · open (Beast Tribe, verified clubs) or private with a code
Groups

Members' own crews. Open in the community or invite only.

Sessions

Hosted by anyone. People join, wait-list, chat.

Courts

Bookable times. A booking becomes a session with the price split.

Challenges + teams

Companies and gyms. Opt-in; totals only.

Captain

Keeps three open sessions a week on the Board.

Member

Can be in several communities and groups. Everyone starts in Beast Tribe.

Tribe feed

Posts and "Beast" reactions, checked and reportable.

5 · How changes reach people

Three speeds of update

Dashboards

Pushed to GitHub, the website rebuilds itself on Vercel.

about 1 min
App update

Most app changes go straight to phones. People close and reopen the app twice.

minutes
New app build

Only for new phone features (health, notifications, location). Goes through TestFlight and Google Play.

hours to a day

Database changes (new tables, rules) are applied separately and take effect at once, for every screen.

6 · Safety and privacy

What the system promises

Rules on every rowA private community's sessions, groups and courts are invisible to outsiders, even to a clever app.
Photos checkedNew photos are checked; a rejected one disappears everywhere at once.
Location stays on the phoneOnly the nearest city is saved, never the exact spot.
Levels are never shownPlayers rate each other privately; it only helps matching.
Health is steps onlyCompanies see team totals, never one person.
Two-step sign-in for staffThe staff dashboard needs it; moderators see only safety pages.

## English

**Short description** (max 80)

> Find a session near you, show up, and train with your tribe.

**Full description**

> Beast Tribe is where training with other people actually happens. Open the app, see what's on today in your city, and join in one tap: a dawn run, a padel game, a football match, a gym class or a ride.
>
> FIND A SESSION
> Sessions near you, today and this week, by sport and level. Join, or take a spot on the waiting list. Every session has its own chat, so you know who's coming and where to meet.
>
> HOST YOUR OWN
> Tap Play, pick the sport, the place and the time, and it's on the board. Booking a court? Say whether it's booked and split the price fairly: everyone sees their share before joining. Going for a run? Pick a track on the map or draw your own.
>
> YOUR PEOPLE
> Join your company's, gym's or club's community with their code, start a group with friends, and meet training partners at your level.
>
> MADE FOR HERE
> English and Arabic, women-only and men-only sessions, and dawn and evening times that fit the day.
>
> By Operation Beast. Free for everyone.

## Data safety

**Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** Yes.
**Is all data encrypted in transit?** Yes.
**Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?** Yes: in the app (You → Settings → Delete account), and by email.

Nothing is shared with third parties (hosting and push are service providers, which Google does not count as sharing). Nothing is sold. Nothing is used for ads.

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Optional? | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | No | Required | App functionality, Account management |
| Email address | Yes | No | Required | Account management |
| User IDs | Yes | No | Required | App functionality, Account management |
| Approximate location | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality (nearest city; the exact position stays on the phone) |
| Photos | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality (profile, posts, session covers) |
| Other in-app messages | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality (session and group chat) |
| Other user-generated content | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality (posts, routes, comments) |
| Fitness info | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality (sports, level, sessions joined) |
| App interactions | Yes | No | Required | App functionality (sessions joined, groups) |
| Other info (gender, date of birth) | Yes | No | Required | App functionality (women-only / men-only sessions, age) |

Do **not** tick: precise location, contacts, calendar, financial info, health info (steps are iPhone-only through Apple Health), web history, device or other IDs, audio, files, crash logs.

# Privacy policy — proposed changes (for approval before publishing)

Live page: https://beast-tribe.vercel.app/legal/privacy (source: `admin/src/app/legal/privacy/page.tsx`).
Why: the policy doesn't yet cover features the app now has. Both stores compare the policy with the app and the privacy answers. A lawyer familiar with Saudi Arabia's PDPL should give it a read before the public release.

## 1. Information We Collect — add these items

- **Training and health information you choose to record** — workouts you finish (time, result, how it felt), meals and water you log in Nutrition, and body measurements (such as weight and body fat) you or your coach record.
- **Communities you join** — the communities (for example your company, club or gym) and packs you belong to.
- **Approximate area you type in** — the city you enter in your profile or on a session. We do not collect your device's location.

## 2. How We Use Your Information — add

- Show your activity to the people you choose: sessions and posts are visible to members of the community or pack they belong to; a private community (for example your company's) is visible only to its members.
- When you share a finished workout, it goes only where you choose: a community feed, a pack chat, or nowhere.
- Count when a coach's workout is completed, so coaches can be paid for their workouts. Coaches see totals, not who completed them.

## New section — Coaches

If you accept a coach's request, you choose what that coach can see (your nutrition log and/or your body measurements). You can change this or stop coaching at any time in the app, and the coach immediately loses access to what you shared.

## New section — Companies, clubs and gyms

If you join an organisation's private community with its invite code, members of that community can see your name, photo, the sessions you join there and what you post there. Operation Beast does not give the organisation access to your nutrition, measurements or workout logs.

## 6. Children's Privacy — keep 13+, add

Some features (for example open chat) are designed for adults; we recommend Beast Tribe for people aged 16 and older.

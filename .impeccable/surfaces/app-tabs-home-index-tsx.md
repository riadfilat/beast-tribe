# Surface brief: Beast Tribe core app (Board, Explore, Session sheet, Host, Tribe, You)

Mode: Operate. Audience: Gulf members finding, joining, and hosting group sessions; Operation Beast community. Job: see what's on today/tomorrow near me, commit in one tap, show up. Constraints: iOS-native navigation (tab bar, stacks, sheets), Arabic + English with RTL, dark and light appearances, works at zero content, OTA-only (Reanimated, SVG, LinearGradient, Haptics, SF Symbols available; no blur/image libs).

Binding brand rules (Operation Beast Guideline, 60 pp.): Deep Teal #023C3C field, Orange #E88F24 as spot color only (CTA, circle), Aqua #56C4C4, Light Gray #F2F0EE; teal text on orange; orange never small text on light; Slam Dunk is hero-only and rare; Futura PT primary (Montserrat stands in); icons solid/sharp, no rounded bubbly sets; orange circle = marker/focal point/highlight numbers; zig-zags as separators; lightning slashes; teal photo overlays 60–80%; journey stages Dreamer (aqua) / Seeker (orange) / Mover (teal); voice motivating not preachy, inclusive, community-driven.

Memorable moment: tapping I'M IN raises the orange sun behind the session's time while your name magnet snaps onto the roster.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The class board every box keeps by the door: today's sessions set at their times, and you're in when your name is on it. Refuses photo-card carousels, stat dashboards, and cards-as-structure.

OWN-WORLD: Deep Teal #023C3C board in dark, Light Gray #F2F0EE board in light; chalk-white ink on teal, teal ink on gray. The orange circle marks only what is yours (joined, hosting), what is live, and the act of joining; aqua for packs, coral for women-only, red strike for cancelled. Straight hairline rules, never cards; zig-zag day dividers; name magnets for people; angular tally strokes for capacity. Slam Dunk only for the hero lines (TODAY, your next time, welcome); Montserrat ExtraBold for times and titles, Noto Kufi Arabic for Arabic titles, SF for everything else. Only real place photography carries full color, under a teal overlay.

STORY: The member opens the board, reads today and tomorrow in time order against a live NOW marker, finds their own session with the orange sun behind its time and the crew's magnets beside it, opens the session sheet, taps I'M IN, and watches the sun rise behind it. An empty board says every beast starts somewhere and invites them to write the first session.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top row: wordmark mark and date, inbox bell, "+ Host". Hero "TODAY" in Slam Dunk. A rail down the leading edge with the orange NOW disc at the current time. Rows hang off the rail: time (next session large, with the sun behind it when it's yours), caps title, sport glyph + place, magnets and tally on the trailing line. Tab bar: Board · Explore · Tribe · You.

FORM: The Box Board (gym-box class board), candidate 6 of my ordered list of 7; seed key 79c15ff2. Signature interaction: the orange sun rises behind the time as your magnet snaps on, with a success haptic. Motion grammar: circles rise with a spring, rules cut in on a dynamic angle, finished sessions dim once; everything else is instant; Reduce Motion shows final states.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
